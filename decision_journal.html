Personal Systems · Decision Journal

Personal Decision Journal

Record one unresolved decision each day. You make the decision; this page helps make your reasoning and predictions testable.

1 · Decision and alternatives

Challenge: have you included the status quo, a reversible trial, and the cost of waiting?

2 · Reasons and counterargument

Give your three strongest reasons, not three versions of the same assumption.

Challenge: what are you treating as fact that is really an estimate, assumption, or preference?

3 · Three measurable predictions

Use observable outcomes, a threshold and a deadline. Example: “By 31 March, I will complete at least 8 training sessions” (70%), not “I will be healthier.” Probabilities express your confidence, not guaranteed outcomes.

4 · Change criteria and review

Fill in the decision to see the clarity checks.

5 · Review the outcome

Original predictions remain unchanged. Judge each against the exact criterion and deadline you wrote when deciding. If the deadline has not arrived, use Pending.

Journal entries

A good decision journal

Separates what you knew when deciding from what you learned afterward. A decision can be well reasoned even when the outcome is unfavorable. Probability forecasts are useful only when the events are unambiguous at review time.

All entries stay in this browser unless you export a backup. This offline page offers structured challenges, not live AI evaluation.
