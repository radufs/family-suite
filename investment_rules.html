Denmark tax guardrail

Keep tax rates and account limits out of the permanent rulebook because they change. At the annual review, verify the current rules on SKAT. In 2026, SKAT states that Aktiesparekonto returns are taxed at 17% under the mark-to-market principle and the contribution ceiling is 174,200 DKK; SKAT also states a 9,900 DKK basic annual aldersopsparing contribution limit and 15.3% annual tax on pension returns. These figures are reference points, not permanent rules.
